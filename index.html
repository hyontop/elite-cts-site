Before you book

Do I need experience to book a course?

No. Novice routes are available for anyone with no prior experience, and experienced operators can take a shorter test-only or refresher route instead. Tell us the operator's background and we'll tell you which route applies.

Do you come to us, or do we come to you?

Either. Training and testing runs at the training facilities we use, or at your own workplace where the necessary machinery is available. On-site delivery is available UK-wide and is usually easier for a crew.

What's the difference between NPORS and CPCS?

Both are recognised UK card schemes proving an operator's competence and health and safety awareness. Which one you need usually comes down to what your site or principal contractor asks for.

How much does a course cost?

It depends on the category, the route, how many people you're putting through and whether we travel to you. Call or email with those details and we'll quote you properly.
